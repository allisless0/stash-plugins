# Insights

A stats dashboard on Stash's **Stats** page, below Stash's own numbers. Five
tabs; every bar, day and row leads into Stash.

## Overview

Today and this week, your streak (and best streak), your record day, and watch
time per O. A year calendar of O's (click a day to open it), O's per week, when
you O by weekday and hour, your top performers, and the start of your backlog.

## Activity

- **O's or watch time** by week, month or year, with earlier and later.
  Click a day to open it, a month in the year view to open that month.
- **Streaks and gaps:** current and best streak with dates, longest break, how
  often on average, record day.
- **A day:** its O's and plays on a 24-hour line and as a list, with how long
  you watched. Plays next to an O of the same scene are folded into it.
- **Top scenes:** most O's, most played, and ones you have not gone back to in
  three months.

## People: what works for you

For each trait, how your O's (or your watching) compare with how much of the
library has it: **1.5x** means it gets you there half again as often as its
share of the library would suggest. Green is above its weight, coral below.

- **Performer traits:** nationality, ethnicity, hair, eyes, height, weight, cup
  size, natural or enhanced, tattoos, piercings, age in the scene, career stage
  in the scene, favourites, and performer tags (so Advanced Rating's star tags
  work too).
- **Scene traits:** tags, studios, cast size, length, resolution, release era,
  interactive.
- **Your strongest pulls** sums up the clearest ones across all of these.
- Groups too small to judge are hidden; show them with the button underneath.

Also: performers by O's, by O's per scene, by time watched, and favourites you
have not had an O with in six months; performers by country with a gender
filter; and the age of performers on the date of each scene.

## Library and backlog

Size and hours, rated and organized shares, release and added-to-Stash
timelines (click a year for its months, a month for its scenes), and O's per
scene by length, resolution, cast and rating.

The backlog lists never watched, watched but not rated, started but not
finished, played three or more times without an O, rated four stars and up
without an O, and not organized. Each opens exactly those scenes.

## Watch time

Counted while a scene's own player is playing, not hover previews or the wall,
and saved into Stash once a minute, so it is the same on every device. If you
used **O Stats**, Insights finds its watch history and offers to import it.

## Good to know

- O's without a date (older Stash versions, imports) count in totals and
  per-scene figures; day charts say how many they could not place.
- The library is read once and kept for 30 minutes; **Refresh** reads it again.
- Everything is computed in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
- Replaces **O Stats** and **Stats Enhancer**: disable both once Insights is
  installed (import O Stats' watch history first).
